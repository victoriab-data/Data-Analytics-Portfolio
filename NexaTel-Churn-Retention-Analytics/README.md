NexaTel Communications — Customer Churn & Retention Analytics
A full-cycle analytics project covering data cleaning, dimensional modeling, and BI dashboard design for a simulated telecom company facing customer churn.
MySQL data cleaning → Power BI star-schema modeling → 4-page interactive dashboard
![Executive Overview](Dashboard_Screenshots/01_Executive_Overview.png)
---
The Business Question
Why are customers leaving NexaTel, what is churn costing the business, and are the company's retention campaigns delivering a positive return?
Highlights
~450,000 records across 4 linked tables, cleaned end-to-end in MySQL
25.41% churn rate identified, with 82.77% of churn concentrated in a customer's first 6 months
$222.73K in revenue lost to churn, against $8.72M saved by retention campaigns (42.92 ROI)
5 mixed date formats resolved with evidence-based logic, not assumption — see the SQL evidence
4-page Power BI dashboard: Executive Overview, Customer & Churn Drivers, Revenue & Customer Value, Retention Campaign Performance
